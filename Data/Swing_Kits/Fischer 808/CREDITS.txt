EON Swing — Fischer 808 factory kits
====================================

The drum samples in these kits are from:

    Roland TR-808 Rhythm Composer Sound Sample Set 1.0.0 (1994)
    Michael Fischer / Technopolis

Recorded from a real Roland TR-808 (serial no. 103852) via the
individual sound outputs, 16-bit / 44.1 kHz, and released by the
author as a free sample set. Every knob position in the original
set was sampled at five uniform positions; these kits use the
documented mid positions (808 F) and long-decay positions
(808 F BOOM).

Full original documentation is preserved in TR808.TXT alongside
this file. All credit for the recordings belongs to Michael
Fischer; EON Studios claims no ownership of the sample content.
